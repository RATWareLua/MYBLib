# JB2008 thermospheric density

**English** | [Русский](jb2008.ru.md) · [All passports](index.en.md)

## What it is

The Jacchia–Bowman 2008 empirical model of the density of the Earth's thermosphere from 90 km
up: the exospheric temperature from four solar indices (F10, S10, M10, Y10) and the storm term
dTc, the static model of Jacchia (temperature profile, composition, barometric and diffusion
equations), the diurnal, seasonal-latitudinal and semiannual variations and the factor above
1000 km. It is a clean implementation from the papers, not a translation of the program of
Space Environment Technologies (SET), in an optional module with readers of SET's index files
SOLFSMY and DTCFILE and an adapter that makes it the atmosphere of a force model. The common
input of the other upper-atmosphere models is in [thermosphere models](thermosphere-models.en.md).

## Source

- B. R. Bowman, W. K. Tobiska, F. A. Marcos, C. Y. Huang, C. S. Lin, W. J. Burke, AIAA
  2008-6438 (the exospheric temperature from F10, S10, M10, Y10; the semiannual variation);
  B. R. Bowman et al., "The JB2006 empirical thermospheric density model", *J. Atmos.
  Solar-Terr. Phys.* 70, 774 (2008) (the corrections of the temperature by local time,
  latitude and height; the factor above 1000 km); L. G. Jacchia, SAO Special Reports 313
  (1970) and 332 (1971) (the static model; the diurnal and seasonal-latitudinal variations).
- The SET licence forbids modifying or translating the program, so no line of it was read.
  What the papers leave open was settled by running the program unchanged, as a black box, on
  the Gentoo VM and comparing its outputs: the J70 constants of the static model and the
  diurnal variation (m = 2.5, R = 0.31); the corrections of JB2006 eq. 7 and 8 in their bands
  (200–240 and 300–600 km), joined by Hermite cubics to zero at 120 and 800 km and to each
  other between 240 and 300 km, with the daily F10; the seasonal-latitudinal term of J70 with
  the time argument of J71; no seasonal-latitudinal variation of helium; the semiannual F(z)
  not below zero.
- The module is under myb's BSD-3; it is not endorsed by, nor affiliated with, SET or the
  authors ([`ext/jb2008/NOTICE`](../../ext/jb2008/NOTICE)). The index files belong to SET and
  are not part of myb.

## Interface

Header [`myb_jb2008.h`](../../ext/jb2008/myb_jb2008.h) (target `myb::jb2008`, not part of
libmyb):

- the model `myb_jb2008`;
- the readers `myb_jb2008_solar_load`, `myb_jb2008_solar_load_file`,
  `myb_jb2008_solar_destroy`, `myb_jb2008_solar_at`, `myb_jb2008_storm_load`,
  `myb_jb2008_storm_load_file`, `myb_jb2008_storm_destroy`, `myb_jb2008_storm_at`;
- the atmosphere of a force model `myb_jb2008_density` (a `myb_density_function` of
  [`myb/force.h`](../../include/myb/force.h));
- structures `myb_jb2008_indices`, `myb_jb2008_output`, `myb_jb2008_atmosphere`; opaque
  tables `myb_jb2008_solar`, `myb_jb2008_storm`.

## Inputs and outputs

- `myb_jb2008`: the MJD (UT); the right ascension and declination of the Sun (rad); the
  right ascension of the place (the sidereal angle plus the east longitude, rad), its
  geocentric latitude (rad) and height (km). The indices are already lagged: F10 and S10 of
  the day before, M10 of two days before, Y10 of five days before, each with its 81-day mean
  centred on the same day (sfu), and dTc in K. Output: the density in kg m⁻³, the exospheric
  temperature above the place and the temperature at the place in K.
- SOLFSMY.TXT: `#` comments, one record per UTC day "YYYY DDD JulianDay F10 F81c S10 S81c M10
  M81c Y10 Y81c Ssrc". DTCFILE.TXT: "DTC YYYY DDD" and the 24 hourly values of that UTC day
  (K). The days must follow one another without gaps. `myb_jb2008_solar_at` gives the eight
  solar indices of the day lagged as above (dTc is left as it is); `myb_jb2008_storm_at` the
  dTc of the UTC hour.
- `myb_jb2008_density`: the body-fixed place r (m) at the instant t (on `scale`) → kg m⁻³.
  The UTC day and hour choose the indices of the tables (or `fixed` at all times); the
  latitude is geocentric, the height over the ellipsoid; the Sun is the low-precision Sun of
  the Astronomical Almanac (0.01°), the right ascension of the place GMST 1982 on UTC plus the
  longitude — the hour angle of the Sun, which the model uses, to about 0.01°.

## Domain of validity

- From 90 km up; compared with the SET program from 90 to 6000 km (one comment of the header
  says 4000 km).
- `myb_jb2008` refuses with `MYB_E_DOMAIN`: input that is not finite, a height below 90 km,
  negative indices.
- Readers: `MYB_E_FORMAT` (a record that is not one, a gap between days; `diag` gives the
  line), `MYB_E_NOMEM`, `MYB_E_IO`. `myb_jb2008_solar_at` and `myb_jb2008_storm_at`:
  `MYB_E_RANGE` for a day or hour the table does not hold (the lags need the five days
  before).
- The adapter: the errors of `myb_timesys_to_utc`, `myb_geodetic_from_cartesian`, the tables
  and the model.

## Accuracy

| What | Reference | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Static model at T∞ = 1000 K, 90–2500 km | Jacchia 1970, Table 5 | T 0.049 K, log₁₀ ρ 1.1·10⁻³; log n of N₂, O₂, O, Ar, H within 3·10⁻⁴, of He 1.5·10⁻³ (the table's helium is 0.0012 above its own equations) |
| Exospheric temperature, 655 inputs, 90–6000 km | the SET program run unchanged | **1.1·10⁻⁶ K** |
| Temperature at the place | the same | 2·10⁻⁵ K |
| log₁₀ ρ | the same | 8.9·10⁻⁴ rms (0.2 %), 4.8·10⁻³ at worst (1.1 %) |
| The adapter: 420 km over 51.6° N, 30° E, 2005-01-05 07:12 UTC | `myb_jb2008` with the Almanac Sun and GMST 1982 worked out in the test | within 10⁻⁶ |
| The readers and the lags | files made in the published formats | exact |
| The temperature through the joins at 120, 200, 240, 300, 600, 800 km | continuity | jumps below 10⁻⁴ K |

The remaining difference in density is set by the three significant digits of the semiannual
coefficients and the four of the factor above 1000 km as the papers print them, and by the
integration (Gauss–Legendre here; the program's Newton–Cotes steps are not published). Also
checked: dTc adds to the exospheric temperature exactly (10⁻⁹ K), the density falls with the
height from 90 to 4000 km.

Test: `test_jb2008` ([tests/ext/test_jb2008.c](../../tests/ext/test_jb2008.c)). Without the
user's files it runs the static model against Jacchia's table, the readers on files made in
the test, the properties and the adapter. The comparison with the SET program runs only when
`data/download/jb2008_set_outputs.txt` is there — made locally by running the program, never
distributed and not in `cmake/FetchData.cmake`.

## Cost

Not measured.

## Limitations

- Not the SET program: the density differs from it by the amounts above (at worst 1.1 %).
- The index files of SET may not be redistributed: the user obtains SOLFSMY and DTCFILE from
  SET under its terms.
- The adapter's Sun is good to 0.01° and the sidereal time is GMST 1982 on UTC, not UT1: the
  hour angle of the Sun is good to about 0.01°.
- Specific to the Earth.

## Data

- No data files in the repository. The indices come from the user's copies of SOLFSMY.TXT and
  DTCFILE.TXT (Space Environment Technologies, under its licence).
- The adapter needs a time system with UTC: `data/time/time-scales.tk`, `tdb-tt-fb1990.tk`,
  `utc-tai.tk`.
- Optional: `data/download/jb2008_set_outputs.txt`, outputs of the SET program made locally.
